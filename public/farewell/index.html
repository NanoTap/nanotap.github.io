Farewell, NanoTap is now a public archive.

Nearly four months ago we released NanoTap. The idea was simple, easy app publishing and installation.

A few days later, we opened up submitions via a link on the Discord server. We didn't expect many users but we did not get any submitions at all. We kept Nanotap open and looked into other projects.

That was when we decided to make a wiki for Apple Internal. We made the Discord server, put it on Disboard and got some interest. NanoTap stayed outdated without the code even being released.

Farewell to NanoTap...
